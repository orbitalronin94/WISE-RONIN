# WISE-RONIN cleaned master corpus

This directory is generated by **WISE-RONIN — Unified master dataset build**.

## Training master

The trainable master combines:
- The deterministic 80,000-row balanced factual and arithmetic corpus.
- Retained QA Ronin records that are not flagged for manual review.
- The 20 concise antisycophancy examples from the legacy shards.

The builder preserves source metadata and adds `source_dataset` / `source_line`. Exact normalized prompt-and-answer duplicates are removed. QA entries with factual, temporal, or ambiguous-answer problems are quarantined in `wise_ronin_qa_manual_review.jsonl`; 33 records are held for manual review.

## Legacy material excluded from general SFT

The historical shards contain two large template-heavy classes that must not be mixed into ordinary factual instruction tuning:

- `hecho_rodeo_trolleo`: generated answers repeatedly attach arbitrary objects, numbers, and rhetorical boilerplate to factual questions.
- `koan_hecho`: KOAN-style templates contaminate factual answers and sometimes damage the question text itself.

The current build excludes **125,377 raw legacy rows** from the trainable master: 88,345 `hecho_rodeo_trolleo` and 37,032 `koan_hecho`. All excluded rows, with original provenance and an exclusion reason, are retained in `wise_ronin_legacy_excluded.jsonl` for audit or isolated style experiments. The original three source shards are not edited or deleted.

The expected clean training master is approximately 81k rows: 80k balanced factual/math examples, retained manual QA, and 20 antisycophancy examples. The build validates that neither excluded legacy class leaks into the master.

## Reproducibility and boundaries

- The balanced corpus is regenerated deterministically from the pinned expanded source.
- Original source shards, QA source records, and holdout files remain unchanged.
- The workflow only builds, validates, and uploads datasets; it does not launch training.
- The output artifact is `wise-ronin-clean-master-dataset`.

See `wise_ronin_master_report.json`, `wise_ronin_prompt_variants.jsonl`, `wise_ronin_qa_manual_review.jsonl`, and `wise_ronin_legacy_excluded.jsonl` for the machine-readable audit trail.
