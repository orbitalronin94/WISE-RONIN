# WISE-RONIN unified master corpus

This directory is generated by the manual workflow **WISE-RONIN — Unified master dataset build**.

It combines:
- The three historical `dataset_enano_70mb_parte*.jsonl` shards.
- The deterministic 80,000-row balanced corpus built from `data/expanded/wise_ronin_expanded.jsonl`.
- The 976 records in `data/seeds/wise_ronin_1000_qa_ronin.jsonl`.

The build preserves `fact_id`, `variant_id`, `tipo`, and all other source metadata, while adding `source_dataset` and `source_line`. It collapses exact normalized-prompt duplicates with identical answers. Prompt collisions with different answers are quarantined rather than resolved arbitrarily.

Two uploaded QA entries are held out for manual review: the claim about the second-best electrical conductor and the unqualified question about Israel's capital. They remain in the source file and appear in `wise_ronin_qa_manual_review.jsonl`, but are not included in the trainable master until reviewed.

The workflow is manual-only. It does not launch training, alter the historical shards, or touch any holdout. The generated corpus and reports are published as the Actions artifact `wise-ronin-unified-master-dataset`.
